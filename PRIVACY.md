# RedeemDeck Privacy Policy / RedeemDeck 隐私政策

Last updated: September 4, 2026

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 4 日

## English

RedeemDeck is an open-source, local-first utility for organizing App Store offer codes and promotional codes. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or a developer-operated backend.

### Data storage

Apps, categories, codes, workflow states, greetings, and settings are stored locally on your device. RedeemDeck does not upload this library to the developer. RedeemDeck does not use CloudKit or another cross-device synchronization service.

Backup files are created only when you request an export. They contain complete, unmasked codes and are not additionally encrypted by RedeemDeck. You choose where to save or share them and are responsible for keeping them secure.

### Network access

When you ask RedeemDeck to look up an App Store app, the app sends the App Store identifier to Apple's public lookup service to retrieve public metadata and artwork. Opening a redemption link or App Store page sends that request directly to Apple. Apple's handling of those requests is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

RedeemDeck does not send your stored code library to the developer or to a developer-operated server.

### Permissions

- Photo Library add access is requested only when you choose to save a QR poster.
- Notification permission is requested only when you enable expiration reminders.
- File access is initiated by you when importing CSV files or exporting and restoring backups.

### Retention and deletion

You can delete individual records in the app. Deleting RedeemDeck removes its local app container and locally stored library. Backup files saved elsewhere remain under your control and must be deleted separately.

## 简体中文

RedeemDeck 是一款用于整理 App Store 优惠码和促销兑换码的开源、本地优先工具。它不需要账号，不包含广告、分析、用户跟踪，也不使用开发者运营的后端服务。

### 数据存储

App、分类、兑换码、处理状态、海报祝福语和设置均保存在你的设备本地。RedeemDeck 不会将兑换码库上传给开发者，也不使用 CloudKit 或其他跨设备同步服务。

只有在你主动导出时，RedeemDeck 才会创建备份文件。备份包含完整且未遮挡的兑换码，RedeemDeck 不会对备份额外加密。保存和分享位置由你选择，请自行妥善保管。

### 网络访问

当你主动查询 App Store 中的 App 时，RedeemDeck 会将 App Store ID 发送给 Apple 的公开查询服务，以获取公开的 App 信息和图标。打开兑换链接或 App Store 页面时，请求会直接发送给 Apple，相关数据由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理。

RedeemDeck 不会将你保存的兑换码库发送给开发者或开发者运营的服务器。

### 权限

- 只有在你选择保存二维码海报时，才会申请向照片图库添加内容的权限。
- 只有在你启用到期提醒时，才会申请通知权限。
- 导入 CSV、导出备份和恢复备份时，文件访问均由你主动发起。

### 保留与删除

你可以在 App 内删除单条记录。删除 RedeemDeck 会移除其本地 App 容器和本地兑换码库；已经保存到其他位置的备份文件仍由你管理，需要另行删除。
