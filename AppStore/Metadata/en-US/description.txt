RedeemDeck is a free, open-source, local-first offer-code manager for Apple platform developers.

Import offer-code and promotional-code CSV exports from App Store Connect, organize codes by app and offer, and retrieve exactly the quantity you need—without maintaining another spreadsheet.

BUILT FOR THE DISTRIBUTION WORKFLOW

• Organize multiple apps, subscriptions, discounts, campaigns, and other offers
• Keep Available, Pending, Sent, and Expired codes clearly separated
• Select the earliest-expiring inventory automatically
• Copy complete codes or ready-to-redeem links with one tap
• Share a prepared selection with the system share sheet
• Create polished QR posters with app artwork, expiration details, and a personal greeting
• Search, filter, edit, archive, and restore codes from a dedicated management view
• Schedule optional local expiration reminders
• Export and restore complete backups when you choose
• Use the app in English or Simplified Chinese on iPhone and iPad

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No account. No advertising. No analytics or tracking. No developer-operated backend. Your library stays in the app's local sandbox. Network access is limited to public App Store metadata and artwork lookup, plus links that you explicitly open.

“Sent” is a local workflow state. It does not claim that Apple has confirmed a code as redeemed.
